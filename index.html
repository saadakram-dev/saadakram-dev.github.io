One-click multi-network export

Smooth on low-end phones

Redraws only what changes, caps the pixel ratio and pre-renders static layers, so playables stay smooth on cheap Android devices.

Animation and sound

  1. Brief and mechanic

    I pick the one mechanic that sells your game and plan the hook, play time and end card around it.

  2. Build

    Unity with Luna Playworks, or hand-coded HTML5 when a lighter build is the better fit.

  3. Optimize

    Under 5 MB, fast first frame, and smooth on low-end Android phones.

  4. Export and QA

    Network-specific builds, MRAID checks and device testing, so review passes the first time.
